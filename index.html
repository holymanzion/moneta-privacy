Privacy Policy

Moneta: Budget & Money Tracker, by Endinov

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Moneta ("the app") is a personal money manager made by Endinov ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Moneta.

The short version

  • Your financial data stays on your phone. Moneta has no account system and no server of its own that stores your data.
  • AI features are optional and off by default. If you turn them on, the specific text needed for a request is sent to Google's Gemini AI through Firebase.
  • We don't sell your data or show ads.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored only in a database on your phone: accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, savings goals, bills, receipt photos, saved AI chats and your settings. It is not sent to us. If you delete the app or use Settings → Erase all data, it is deleted from your phone.

Backups you create with Settings → Back up everything are files you save or share yourself, for example to Google Drive or email. Once shared, they are handled by the service you chose. Backups don't include receipt photos.

Camera and photos

Moneta uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to scan or attach a receipt. The text on the receipt is read on your phone using Google ML Kit, which works offline. Receipt photos are not uploaded.

AI features (optional)

The AI assistant is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it is on:

  • Smart quick add sends the note you type (for example "lunch 45 cash") together with your category and account names, so the AI can fill in a transaction.
  • Ask your money and Monthly review send a summary worked out on your phone: account balances, monthly and category totals, budgets, goals, bills, and recent transactions (date, amount, category, account and payee). Transaction notes and receipt photos are never sent. Moneta asks for your permission before sending this the first time.

These requests are processed by Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic. Google handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We do not store your requests or the AI's answers on any server; saved chats stay on your phone (only the 10 most recent are kept).

You can turn AI off at any time in Settings → AI assistant.

App integrity checks

To protect the AI features from abuse, Moneta uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity. This lets Google confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. It does not give us access to your financial data.

Moneta Pro subscriptions

Moneta Pro is sold through Google Play, and Google handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details. To check whether you have Pro, Moneta uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipt from Google Play and an anonymous app user ID. No financial data from Moneta is sent to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

To count the free plan's AI limits, Moneta keeps a simple usage counter on your phone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Moneta schedules bill reminders on your phone. No reminder data is sent anywhere.

Security

You can protect Moneta with a PIN and, on supported phones, fingerprint or face unlock. The PIN is stored only as a one-way hash on your phone.

Children

Moneta is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
